1. Getting Access

The portal is a web page — nothing to install, works on a phone or computer.

Open the Request Portal

There are two ways to sign in:

  • Google sign-in — use your district account. Try this first.
  • District email + access code — if Google sign-in is blocked (some districts don't allow third-party apps to connect to staff accounts), enter any @arlingtonisd.org email address along with the access code we provided.
Either way, your account follows you. Sign in on your phone and your computer with the same email and you'll see the same requests and invoices on both. Nothing is tied to one device.

Your team can share the access code. Each person signs in with their own @arlingtonisd.org address and sees their own requests — the code unlocks the door, it doesn't merge everyone together.

2. Submitting a Request

Open the portal, stay on the New Request tab, and fill in:

  1. Event name — whatever you call it internally is fine.
  2. Venue — which facility, and the room or space if it matters.
  3. Date
  4. Crew call time — when you need crew on site and working, not when doors open.
  5. Estimated end — your best guess. This sets how long crew are booked, so see section 6 before you round up.
  6. Positions — tap + or − for each role.
  7. Your contact info
  8. Notes — load-in details, special gear, anything unusual.
Submitting isn't a confirmation. It comes to us as a request. We'll check availability and confirm — you'll see the status change in the portal.

How much notice we need

The earlier the better, honestly. Two weeks or more and we can almost always staff it fully. Inside a week gets harder, especially for specialized positions. Same-week requests we'll do our best with, but we may not fill every role.

If something is urgent, submit it anyway and then call — don't wait on the portal.

3. Which Positions to Request

If you're not sure what you need, give your best guess and describe the event in the notes. We'll follow up and adjust — it's easier for us to right-size it than for you to guess exactly.

PositionWhat they do
StagehandCarpentry, stage plots, moving scenery and gear during the show
Spotlight OperatorFollow spot

Rule of thumb: every department you're running (audio, lighting, video) needs a Lead. Assists scale with how big the show is. Stagehands scale with how much moves.

4. Tracking Your Request

Open the My Requests tab any time to see where things stand.

Received We have it and are confirming crew availability.
Confirmed Crew are booked. You're on the schedule.
Completed Event wrapped. Invoice follows once hours are confirmed.
Declined We weren't able to staff it. We'll always reach out directly if this happens.

You'll also hear from us directly — the portal is there so you can check without having to ask.

5. Invoices & Payment

After the event, we review the crew's actual clock-in and clock-out times. Once hours are confirmed, the invoice appears under that event in My Requests.

Each invoice shows:

  • Each position billed, by rate class
  • Hours billed, broken into straight time, overtime, and double time
  • The hourly rate applied
  • Line totals and the amount due

Terms are Net 30 from the invoice date.

Invoices are based on actual worked hours, not estimates — with the minimums and rules in the next section applied.

6. How Hours Are Billed

Worth understanding before you book, because a few of these affect what you're charged more than people expect.

Minimum calls

Every position carries a minimum. If an event finishes early, the minimum still applies — crew turned down other work to hold the date.

PositionMinimum
Stagehand5 hours
General AV / Assist5 hours
Spotlight Operator10 hours
Coordinator10 hours
Lead Technician10 hours
Graphics Operator10 hours
Producer / Director10 hours

Overtime

  • Time and a half after 8 hours for stagehands and assists; after 10 hours for all other positions.
  • Double time after 12 hours, all positions.
  • Night differential: hours between midnight and 6:00 AM bill at time and a half.
Scheduled overtime is billed even if the event ends early. When you book a call that runs into overtime, we commit those hours to the crew and can't take them back. Please give us your realistic end time rather than padding it.

Meals

Meal breaks are scheduled every 5 hours and are paid.

If a meal isn't provided within 5 hours, hours bill at time and a half until crew are released for one. This is the single easiest thing to avoid, and the most common reason an invoice comes in higher than expected. Plan a meal break into any call longer than five hours.

Once a meal is taken, a minimum amount of paid time after it is guaranteed — 3 hours for stagehands, 5 hours for assists.

7. Changes & Cancellations

Plans change — just tell us as early as you can.

  • Adding positions: submit a new request for the same event, and note in the comments that it's an addition.
  • Reducing or changing times: contact us directly. Don't submit a second request — it'll read as a separate call.
  • Cancelling: reach out as soon as you know. Once crew are booked and the date is close, minimums may still apply, and we'd rather talk it through than surprise you on an invoice.
